7# Comment line must start with "#", no space at front.

#

E# matches the (user, host) combination, or, in case of non-networked

J# permissions field of that table entry determines whether the login will
# be accepted or refused.

J# Format of the login access control table is three fields separated by a
# ":" character:

J# The first field should be a "+" (access granted) or "-" (access denied)
